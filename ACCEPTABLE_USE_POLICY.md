# Acceptable Use Policy

Rex-BOF is intended only for good-faith security research and authorized testing.

Use it only against systems you own or have explicit, current, written
authorization to test. That authorization should cover the targets, techniques,
time window, credentials, payloads, and possible service impact.

Do not use Rex-BOF to:

- gain or maintain unauthorized access;
- deploy malware or destructive payloads;
- steal credentials or data;
- disrupt services or target systems outside the agreed scope; or
- conceal activity from the system owner or evade law enforcement.

Follow applicable law, the rules of engagement, data-handling requirements,
safety controls, and notification procedures. Keep production credentials out
of examples, fixtures, issues, and logs.

Stop if scope is uncertain, a safety control fails, cleanup cannot be verified,
or an unintended system is affected. Preserve audit records and follow the
engagement's incident and recovery procedures.

Maintainers may decline support for activity that appears unsafe or
unauthorized. This policy does not add conditions to the
[MIT License](LICENSE).
